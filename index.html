This Diwali, I wish your heart glows brighter than every firework in the sky. You make our festival complete, and nothing would make me happier than seeing you safe and smiling when the last spark fades.

  • 🏞️Celebrate outdoors, in an open space away from houses and vehicles.
  • 💧Keep a bucket of water close by, just in case.
  • 📏Light one cracker at a time, from arm's length, and step back quickly.
  • 👶Keep little ones and pets at a safe distance. Elders can enjoy the show from a comfy chair.
  • 👕Wear cotton clothes and never keep crackers in your pockets.
  • 🚫Never relight a dud. Douse it in water and leave it.

Let the sky sparkle while we stay safe. Happy Diwali, ❤️
